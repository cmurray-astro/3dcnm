    Full native-resolution (NSIDE=1024, 955,629 sightlines) point cloud with a dropdown to colour the points by any catalog quantity: distance, peculiar velocity, TB,CNM, line width, N(HI)CNM, (dAV/dd)peak, cloud depth, or AV,CNM.

    Same explorer, full native-resolution expanded catalog (N_CNM=1, N_dust≤2, BFS-matched), with a toggleable overlay: reconstructed core-collapse supernovae (Swiggum et al. 2026) selected as plausible current drivers of the Local Bubble shell -- dynamically active (age less than the single-remnant Sedov-Taylor stall time) and within that remnant's own current reach of the nearest shell segment (n0=0.3 cm-3). N=4,573 events, median age 6.0 Myr, colour and size both driven by local source density (checkbox to hide).
